# video2post Promo Visual Design

## Style Prompt

A cinematic local-creator workstation for a CLI-first AI content tool: deep ink canvas, warm desk light, cool audio waveforms, crisp Markdown and terminal surfaces, and a product UI that feels practical rather than futuristic for its own sake. The motion should feel like a fast, confident pipeline turning scattered video work into organized publishable drafts.

## Colors

- Background: `#070B13`
- Panel: `#101827`
- Panel glass: `rgba(16, 24, 39, 0.72)`
- Foreground: `#F4F7FB`
- Muted text: `#A9B6C8`
- Accent primary: `#8B5CF6`
- Accent secondary: `#38BDF8`
- Warm highlight: `#F0B46A`
- Success: `#34D399`

## Typography

- Headline: Inter, SF Pro Display, Arial, sans-serif
- Code and file labels: JetBrains Mono, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, monospace
- Use heavy headlines, compact body copy, and no negative letter spacing.

## Motion

- Scenes move with quick, overlapping entrances.
- Pipeline nodes should feel sequential and mechanical: each stage locks in, then the next one wakes up.
- Use subtle ambient movement on waveform, grid, and image layers.
- End with a clean product lockup, not a generic call-to-action card.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not make a generic SaaS landing page.
- Do not rely on unrelated stock footage.
- Do not turn the palette into pure cyan-on-black; keep warm desk-light contrast.
- Do not use oversized explanatory paragraphs on screen.
- Do not hide the concrete outputs: `notes.md`, `x_article.md`, `x_thread.md`, `x_titles.md`, `cover.jpg`.
